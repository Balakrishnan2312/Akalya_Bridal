About The Studio

Our Story

With over 10 years of experience, Akalya Bridal Studio has been dedicated to enhancing beauty with professional bridal makeover and beauty parlour services. Our passion is to make every bride feel confident, elegant, and unforgettable on her special day through personalized beauty solutions.

Along with our beauty services, we also specialize in fashion designing and ladies’ tailoring, creating custom outfits that perfectly match every client’s style and occasion. From bridal wear to designer dresses, we combine creativity, craftsmanship, and attention to detail to bring your vision to life.
